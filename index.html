About PluginProof

I'm Aaron, founder of PluginProof. I help audio software developers test and ship better products.

I specialize in exploratory and risk-based testing for audio plugins, using real-world workflows to uncover bugs, compatibility issues, unexpected behavior, and edge cases that scripted testing can miss.

I also provide technical documentation support, helping turn complex audio products into clear, accurate documentation that musicians can actually use.

My approach combines hands-on product testing with a growing technical understanding of DSP, plugin architecture, DAWs, and music production. AI and automation are used where they make the work more efficient, while technical judgment and product understanding remain at the center.

PluginProof exists to provide specialized quality and technical services for audio software companies—helping them improve their products without having to build every capability in-house.
